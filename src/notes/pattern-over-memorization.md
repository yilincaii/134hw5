For a long time, my LeetCode practice was mostly memorizing the solutions to common interview questions. It helped me recognize a few familiar problems, but whenever the question changed slightly, I often felt stuck again.

Recently, I started grouping similar problems instead of treating every question as something completely new. For string problems, I began asking whether the problem looked like a Trie, DFS, stack, sliding window, or graph problem.

This changed the way I read questions. Now, my first goal is not to remember the exact code. I try to identify the problem family and then choose from the tools I already know.

The best part is that getting a question wrong feels less frustrating now. It usually means I found a pattern or technique that was missing from my current toolbox.

I still have a lot to practice, but I finally feel like I am learning how to solve problems instead of only learning how to repeat answers.
